Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

Maison Moi is a personal wardrobe app. Your closet is personal, so we keep this simple: we collect what the app needs to work, we never sell it, and you can take it with you or delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address and password. Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody, including us, can read them.
  • Your profile. Your name, zip code, goals and chosen theme.
  • Your closet. The pieces you add and their details (brand, color, size, price, condition, notes), photos you upload, what you wear, laundry status and the outfits you like or skip.
  • Your inspiration. Links, Pinterest boards and saved looks you add.
  • Your calendar, only if you import one. Event titles, times and locations from a calendar file you choose, used to plan outfits for your day. You can clear it anytime.
  • Your plan. If you subscribe to Maison Moi Plus, Stripe handles your payment as the seller of record. We receive your plan status, trial and renewal dates and a Stripe customer ID. We never see or store your card number.
  • Basic technical information. Like most websites, the services that host Maison Moi may log your IP address and browser type for security and to keep things running.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your closet, recommend outfits for your weather and plans, and sync across your devices.
  • To manage your plan, free trial and billing.
  • To answer you when you contact us, and to keep accounts secure.

We don't sell your information, show you ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers. We don't use your closet to train AI models.

Storage on your device

Maison Moi keeps a copy of your closet in your browser's local storage so it opens instantly and works offline, plus a sign-in token so you stay signed in. Signing out removes them, along with any other Maison Moi data saved in that browser. We don't use advertising cookies.

Who can see your information

Only you, through your account. Security rules in our database stop any account from reading anyone else's closet, and your photos are kept in private storage. Maison Moi's owner can access account data when needed for support, security or legal reasons, and never for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • We keep your information while your account is open.
  • You can delete any piece, photo or calendar import in the app at any time.
  • You can delete your whole account yourself in the app (Profile, then Delete my account). This immediately deletes your account, closet and photos, ends any Plus subscription, and clears the copy on that browser. You can also email us from your account's email address and we'll do it within 30 days. Any copies in backups expire within 30 days after that.
  • Stripe keeps billing records for as long as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • See and take your data: Profile, then Your data, then Export .json gives you a full copy anytime.
  • Correct it: edit anything in the app.
  • Delete it: as described above.

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have extra rights, such as asking what we hold about you. Email us and we'll help. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, and each account's data is walled off from every other account. No system is perfect, so please use a strong password you don't use anywhere else.

Children

Maison Moi isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll let you know in the app or by email first.
